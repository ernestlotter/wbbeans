# Dashboard options

**Tools ▸ Options ▸ Dashboard**:

| Setting | Range | Default |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Refresh every (seconds)** | 1 – 60 | 1 |
| **History (points)** | 10 – 1000 | 120 |
| **Notify on high load** | on / off | on |

Values outside the range are marked under the field and keep *OK* disabled. Changes apply to open dashboards
immediately; the settings are stored for your account.
