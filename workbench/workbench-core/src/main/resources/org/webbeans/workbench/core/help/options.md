# Workbench options

**Tools ▸ Options ▸ Workbench** holds the two settings that change the whole application:

| Setting | Values | Effect |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **Theme** | Follow the system, Light, Dark | Applied to every open tab at once |
| **Language** | English, Deutsch | Menus, windows, dialogs and the status bar relabel themselves without a reload |

Both are remembered for your account and applied again when you sign in next time. *Apply* keeps the dialog
open so you can compare; *OK* applies and closes; *Cancel* leaves everything as it was.

Other categories of the Options dialog belong to the individual tools — see [Dashboard options](../../dashboard/help/dashboard-options.md).
