# Getting around

The application window is made of **windows** docked into areas: an explorer on the left, editors in the
middle, properties on the right and output at the bottom. Everything you see is a window you can move.

## Windows

- Drag a window's tab to another area to move it; drop it next to the tabs of that area.
- Double-click a tab (or use the maximise button) to give the window the whole page; double-click again to restore.
- Close a window with the **×** on its tab; reopen it from the **Window** menu.
- The arrangement is saved for you and restored at the next login. **Window ▸ Reset Layout** puts everything back.

## Menus, toolbars and shortcuts

Commands live in the menu bar and the toolbars; the right-click menu of an item shows the commands that apply to
it. Keyboard shortcuts are shown next to menu entries, for example <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> to save. The
**View ▸ Toolbars** menu shows or hides toolbars.

## The Properties window

The Properties window follows whatever is selected: a file, a part, a chart. Values with a white background can
be edited in place; press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to apply, <kbd>Escape</kbd> to cancel.

## Options

**Tools ▸ Options** collects the settings of every part of the application in one dialog. *Apply* keeps the
dialog open; *OK* applies and closes. Settings are remembered per user.

## Getting help

Press <kbd>F1</kbd> anywhere for the help page of the active window, or open **Help ▸ Help Contents**. Dialogs
with a *Help* button open their own page. The search field at the top of the Help window finds pages by title
and text; the **Contents** button returns to the table of contents, and *Open in new tab* shows the page in a
browser tab of its own.
